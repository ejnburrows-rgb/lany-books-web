The Science of Spanish Literacy

Discover how La Cartilla de Gretel blends time-tested phonetic methods with gentle, modern digital design to create a seamless learning experience for early readers.

Método Silábico

We rely on the proven syllabic method, starting with vowels and progressively introducing consonants to build syllables, words, and eventually confident sentences.

Controlled Phonetics

Vocabulary is carefully curated so children only encounter sounds they have already mastered, preventing frustration and building early reading confidence.

Visual Associations

Rich, memorable illustrations directly map to phonetic sounds, serving as cognitive anchors that accelerate memorization and recall for young learners.
