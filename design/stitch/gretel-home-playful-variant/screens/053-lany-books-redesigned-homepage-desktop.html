A Heritage of Learning

For over five decades, La Cartilla de Gretel has been a cornerstone of early Spanish literacy. From bustling city classrooms to remote forest schools, our method has adapted and thrived, bringing the joy of reading to countless children. Today, we continue that legacy with a renewed commitment to accessible, engaging education.
