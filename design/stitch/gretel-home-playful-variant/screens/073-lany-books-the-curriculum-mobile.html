The 6 Stages

1

Isolation

Introducing the pure sound independent of context, focusing on mouth shape.

2

Syllabic Blending

Merging consonants with the 5 foundational vowels (ma, me, mi, mo, mu).

3

Word Formation

Combining known syllables to form highly recognizable concrete nouns.

4

Short Phrases

Building simple subject-verb constructs using visual cues.

5

Sentences

Expanding to full thoughts with appropriate punctuation introduction.

6

Poetry & Rhythm

Applying mastered sounds to rhythmic, rhyming texts to build fluency.
