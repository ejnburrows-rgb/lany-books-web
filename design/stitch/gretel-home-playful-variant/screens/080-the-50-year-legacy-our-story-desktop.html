Heritage & Impact

The 50-Year Legacy of
La Cartilla de Gretel

For half a century, we have transformed the daunting task of learning to read into an enchanting adventure, touching the lives of millions of children across the globe.

The Origin of an Adventure

Decades ago, Author I. Gretel recognized a profound truth: children learn best when they are captivated. The vision was simple yet revolutionary—to create a world where reading was not a chore, but a thrilling adventure filled with colorful characters and engaging stories.

From its humble beginnings in local classrooms to becoming a staple in educational kits worldwide, La Cartilla de Gretel has maintained its core philosophy. The transition over 50 years has preserved the magic while embracing modern pedagogical needs, always keeping the physical book at the heart of learning.

The Vision of Leonor Lopetegui

Dedicated to the belief that every child deserves the joy of reading, Leonor Lopetegui poured her heart into developing a pedagogy that feels less like a lesson and more like a story unfolding. Her mission was to craft a gentle, encouraging atmosphere within the pages, providing educators and parents with a tool that naturally fosters curiosity and confidence.

Milestones of Literacy

1974

The First Edition

La Cartilla de Gretel is first published, introducing its unique, illustration-heavy approach to local schools.

1990

National Adoption

The method becomes a standard text in elementary curricula across the country, celebrated for its effectiveness.

2010

Global Outreach

Translations and adaptations allow the magic of Gretel to reach classrooms in over 15 countries.

2024

The 50th Anniversary

Celebrating a half-century of literacy with updated, high-quality physical kits that honor the original vision while supporting modern educators.
