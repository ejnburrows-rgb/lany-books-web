The 6-Stage Lesson Progression

Every sound introduced follows a strict, scaffolded path from isolation to fluent reading, utilizing the physical Student Book, Flipchart, and Teacher's Guide.

1

Isolation

Introducing the individual sound and symbol using the interactive Flipchart.

2

Syllables

Combining the consonant with vowels (e.g., ma, me, mi, mo, mu) as detailed in the Teacher's Guide.

3

Words

Blending known syllables into simple words with the Student Book.

4

Sight Words

Integrating essential high-frequency words for fluency through physical activities.

5

Sentences

Constructing meaning with decodable text in the Student Book.

6

Poetry

Applying skills in rhythmic, engaging verses (like "Leo lee") found in the Student Book.
