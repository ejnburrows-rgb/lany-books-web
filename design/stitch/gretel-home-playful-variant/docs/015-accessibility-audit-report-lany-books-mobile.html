Image Accessibility (Alt Text) **Analysis:** The design relies heavily on high-quality photography and CRM screenshots. - **Findings:** - Decorative images (like background flourishes) should have empty `alt=""` attributes. - Information-dense images, specifically the CRM screenshots, require detailed descriptive alt text so non-visual users understand the "Empowering Educators" value proposition. - **Recommendations:** - Add descriptive alt text to the CRM dashboard images, e.g., "Mobile CRM dashboard showing student progress at 75% with a 'Report for Families' button." - Ensure the "La Cartilla de Gretel" book cover has alt text identifying the book title and author. ## 3. Color Contrast & Visual Clarity **Analysis:** The brand uses a palette of Navy Blue (#003366 implied), Soft Blue, and White. - **Findings:** - The primary Navy Blue text on light backgrounds provides excellent contrast (likely exceeding 7:1). - **Risk Area:** Small labels or secondary text in "Soft Blue" may fall below the 4.5:1 ratio required for Level AA compliance. - **Recommendations:** - Audit the "Soft Blue" text usage. If used for body copy or functional labels, ensure it is darkened slightly to meet contrast requirements. ## 4. Touch Targets & Interactive Elements **Analysis:** Mobile-first design with several buttons and toggles. - **Findings:** - Buttons like "Explore The Method" and "View Gallery" appear to meet the 44x44px minimum touch target size. - The language and theme toggles in the menu state need explicit `aria-label` attributes (e.g., "Switch to Spanish," "Toggle Dark Mode") as they are primarily icon-based. - **Recommendations:** - Add `aria-current="page"` to the active navigation link. - Ensure all buttons have a visible `:focus-visible` state for keyboard/switch users. --- ## Action Plan 1. **Immediate:** Update all informative images with descriptive `alt` tags. 2. **Structural:** Wrap navigation and main content in appropriate landmark roles. 3. **Refinement:** Verify contrast ratios for secondary blue text and adjust hex codes if necessary.
