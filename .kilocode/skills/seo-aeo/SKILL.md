# SEO + AEO Pass
1. Every page: exactly one h1; title tag under 60 chars stating service + city
   ("Plumbing repair in Austin — Otto Plumbing"); meta description under 155
   chars with the outcome + a reason to click.
2. Add structured data (JSON-LD): LocalBusiness (name, address, phone, hours)
   or Product for the book. This is what AI assistants and Google quote.
3. Every image: descriptive alt text.
4. AEO: add an FAQ section marked up with FAQPage schema — questions phrased
   the way people ask assistants ("how much does a plumber cost in ___").
   AI answer engines lift these verbatim; that's free placement.
5. Generate sitemap.xml and robots.txt; submit note for Google Search Console
   (free) in the README.
6. Speed: run a Lighthouse check; flag anything under 85 with the fix.
Output: SEO_REPORT.md — what was added per page, what I must do manually
(Search Console signup is a me-task).
