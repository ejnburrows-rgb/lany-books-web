# Landing Copy
Input: who the business serves, what they sell, what makes them different
(ask me these three if missing — lettered options).

OUTPUT, in page order:
1. HERO: headline (max 8 words, states the outcome the visitor wants, not the
   service name) + subline (how, in one sentence) + button text (verb-first:
   "Book my appointment", never "Submit").
2. PAIN: 3 bullets naming the visitor's actual problem in their own words.
3. HOW IT WORKS: 3 numbered steps, each under 10 words.
4. PROOF: template slots for 2 testimonials + the trust line (years, count of
   customers, credentials — ask which exists).
5. FAQ: the 5 questions that stop people from buying, answered honestly.
6. FINAL CTA: restate outcome + button.
RULES: grade-6 reading level. No "solutions", "seamless", "elevate". Every
section must survive the question "would a tired person on a phone keep
reading?" Write for one person, not "customers."
